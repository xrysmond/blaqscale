01
WHAT BLAQSCALE IS

BlaqScale is not an agency. Not a Google Ads vendor. Not a freelancer on a retainer. BlaqScale enters this partnership as a revenue operator — fully invested in the growth of Xouffeur Driver's business with skin in the game.

The mandate is simple: bring money in the door. Channel selection, asset creation, funnel architecture, paid ads, landing pages, booking systems — all of it is determined by what the business actually needs, not by a predefined service package. If something needs to be built to generate revenue, BlaqScale builds it.

The only performance metric that matters is money received in Xouffeur Driver's account. Not traffic. Not leads. Not impressions. Cash in.

02
SCOPE OF WORK

BlaqScale will identify and execute whatever revenue-generating activities the business requires. This may include, but is not limited to:

  • Paid advertising campaigns — Meta Ads, Google Ads, or any channel determined to be effective for Xouffeur Driver's specific acquisition model
  • Landing pages and conversion-optimized web assets built to turn traffic into confirmed bookings
  • Booking system integration and funnel architecture designed to reduce friction between interest and payment
  • Ongoing optimization of all active revenue channels based on real booking and revenue data

All assets built by BlaqScale — landing pages, booking systems, funnels, ad creatives, and any other deliverable — are provided to Xouffeur Driver at no additional cost beyond the terms in Section 03. These assets transfer to Xouffeur Driver upon delivery.

04
PERFORMANCE METRIC & ATTRIBUTION

Revenue is considered BlaqScale-attributed when a booking or payment originates from any asset, campaign, channel, or funnel built or managed by BlaqScale.

  • A booking is attributed to BlaqScale if the customer first engaged with Xouffeur Driver through a BlaqScale-built asset — landing page, ad, funnel, or any other channel activated under this partnership
  • Attribution window: 90 days from the customer's first point of contact through a BlaqScale channel. Any revenue received within this window is commissionable at 15%
  • Attribution holds regardless of the channel through which the booking ultimately closes — WhatsApp, phone, or direct — as long as first contact was through a BlaqScale asset
  • The following are excluded from commission scope: existing Xouffeur Driver clients prior to this agreement; referrals sourced entirely independently; repeat clients not re-acquired through BlaqScale activity

Dispute process: Any attribution dispute will be resolved by referencing tracking data, booking records, and first-contact timestamps held by both parties. If no resolution is reached within 7 days, both parties agree to review source records together and resolve in good faith.

CONFIDENTIALITY

Both parties agree to keep all shared information strictly confidential throughout and after the term of this agreement. This includes:
