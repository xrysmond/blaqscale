01
THE PARTNERSHIP

This is not a vendor-client arrangement. This is a revenue partnership. BlaqScale operates as a revenue operator inside Sawaed Luxury Cars Dubai — using whatever channels, systems, and infrastructure are required to bring measurable, attributable bookings to the business.

BlaqScale is not paid for clicks, views, impressions, or effort. BlaqScale is paid for one thing only — money in the bank for Sawaed Luxury Cars Dubai.

02
SCOPE OF WORK

BlaqScale operates as a full revenue operator for Sawaed Luxury Cars Dubai. The exact execution is determined by BlaqScale based on what the business needs at any given time — not locked to a fixed set of deliverables.

This may include any combination of the following, at BlaqScale's discretion:

  • Paid advertising — Google Ads, Meta Ads, or any other platform best suited to the goal
  • Landing pages — built and optimized to convert traffic into confirmed bookings
  • Booking systems — if required to streamline the path from inquiry to confirmed rental
  • Multi-language targeting — reaching high-intent audiences in Arabic, English, and any other relevant language where demand exists, across all active platforms
  • Tracking and attribution — every lead and booking traced to its source, with full client visibility
  • Ongoing optimization — BlaqScale adjusts strategy and execution continuously based on live performance data

BlaqScale determines what gets built and when. The client provides access, transparency, and cooperation. The goal is singular: 40 confirmed bookings per month.

03
THE GOAL

The agreed performance target is 40 confirmed customer bookings per month, generated through BlaqScale-operated campaigns and funnels.

This is the benchmark against which the commitment fee becomes due. BlaqScale works toward this target from the moment this agreement is signed. There is no fixed timeline — the commitment fee triggers when the goal is achieved.

04
COMPENSATION
COMMITMENT FEE 10,000 AED — due upon hitting 40 bookings/month
REVENUE SHARE 15% of all revenue generated through BlaqScale campaigns
MARKUP REVENUE 100% of any listing markup above base price
PAYMENT CYCLE 1st of each month — and upon immediate demand by BlaqScale
PAYMENT METHOD USDT (Cryptocurrency)
Wallet address provided with each invoice
PAYMENT DEADLINE Within 3 days of invoice — no exceptions

The 15% revenue share is active from day one and applies to all revenue attributable to BlaqScale campaigns. The commitment fee triggers when 40 bookings per month is achieved and confirmed.

Markup Model: Sawaed Luxury Cars Dubai provides BlaqScale with a base rental price per vehicle. BlaqScale may list any vehicle at a higher price at its discretion. If a vehicle rents at BlaqScale's listed price, BlaqScale retains 100% of the markup difference (listed price minus base price) as additional compensation — in addition to the standard 15% revenue share. Example: base price 2,500 AED, listed at 2,700 AED, rented at 2,700 AED — BlaqScale keeps 200 AED markup plus 15% of the 2,700 AED booking.

05
ASSET OWNERSHIP

All digital assets built by BlaqScale under this agreement — including the landing page, booking system, ad account structures, funnel infrastructure, tracking systems, and creative assets — remain the sole property of BlaqScale until the 10,000 AED commitment fee is paid in full.

  • Upon payment of the commitment fee, all assets built by BlaqScale transfer fully to Sawaed Luxury Cars Dubai
  • Prior to payment, the client has operational use of these assets — but does not own them
  • If this agreement is terminated before the commitment fee is paid, BlaqScale retains full ownership of all assets and may remove access to them
06
CLIENT RESPONSIBILITIES

  • Maintain complete transparency on all bookings generated through BlaqScale campaigns — total bookings, confirmed revenue, and customer data

07
ATTRIBUTION & COMMISSION SCOPE

The 15% commission applies to bookings and revenue directly generated and attributable to BlaqScale campaigns and funnels. The following are excluded from commission scope:

  • Existing clients of Sawaed Luxury Cars Dubai prior to this agreement
  • Bookings sourced entirely through independent channels with no connection to BlaqScale campaigns

Attribution Window: A lead is BlaqScale-attributed for 90 days from first contact through a BlaqScale funnel. Any booking that closes within this window is commissionable at 15%.

Repeat Clients: Repeat bookings from clients originally acquired through BlaqScale campaigns are commissionable at 15% for 12 months from the date of their first BlaqScale-attributed booking. After 12 months, they are considered established clients and are no longer commissionable.

Referrals: A customer originally acquired through a BlaqScale campaign who refers another customer is considered a BlaqScale-attributed referral. Bookings from such referrals are commissionable at 15% if they occur within 12 months of the referring customer's first booking.

Dispute process: If attribution is disputed, both parties refer to tracking data as the source of truth. Disputes must be resolved within 7 days through good-faith review of the data.

TERMS & TERMINATION
  • This agreement is active from the date of signing by both parties
  • Either party may terminate with 14 days written notice
  • Any BlaqScale-attributed lead in active contact or negotiation prior to termination that converts within 60 days after termination remains commissionable at 15%
  • BlaqScale is not responsible for bookings lost due to Sawaed Luxury Cars Dubai's failure to respond to leads, fulfill bookings, or maintain operational standards
  • Ad spend is the sole financial responsibility of Sawaed Luxury Cars Dubai and is entirely separate from BlaqScale's commission
10
CONFIDENTIALITY

Both parties agree to keep all shared information strictly confidential. This includes:

  • Internal business data, revenue figures, client names, pricing, and booking volumes shared by either party
  • Account credentials, campaign structure, funnel architecture, and conversion data
  • The existence and terms of this agreement

Neither party shall disclose confidential information to any third party without prior written consent. This obligation survives the termination of this agreement.
